1. S  
   1. SAFETY  
      1. This includes advanced safety features, safe vehicles types like midsize luxury SUVs, midsize SUVs, and midsize cars, and brands deemed to be safe overall (brand level).This is higher the newer the year is  
      2. This is lower for sports cars, subcompact suvs/crossovers/sedans. This is lower the older the year is.  
2. P  
   1. PERFOMRANCE  
      1. This is mainly sport oriented, motor size, acceleration. This is higher for sports cars, performance oriented electric vehicles / full size trucks, or brand level performance boosts  
      2. This is lower for compact/subcompact/crossover  
3. A  
   1. APPEARANCE  
      1. This is mainly cosmetic, contemporary designs, high end brands, sports cars, full size suvs/trucks. This is higher the newer the year is  
      2. This is lower for economy brands, compact designs, van designs, etc. This is lower the older the year is.  
4. C  
   1. COMFORT/CONVENIENCE/COMFORT  
      1. This is creature comforts, tech features (phone hookups), sunroof, leather, heated seat type features. This is higher on midsize/fullsize and higher end brands. This is also higher relatively on newer cars and lower relatively on older cars.  
5. E  
   1. ECONOMY  
      1. This is combination of price and fuel. Electric vehicles will be high economy even though they are higher priced. Compact sedans, crossovers are higher.  
      2. Sports cars, full size suvs, trucks are lower  
6. D  
   1. DEPENDABILITY  
      1. This is long term maintenance. High end brands tend to be lower dependability. Economy brands tend to be higher dependability although some economy brands trade dependability for lower cost.

Most customers default to 1 or 2 nonnegotiables. The rest can be any combination of want or pass.
