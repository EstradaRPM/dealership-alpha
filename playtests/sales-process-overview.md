Sales Process

1) Relationship and Trust \- “Sell Yourself”  
   1) Welcome (first impression, introduction)  
   2) Understand Goals (needs assessment, customer interview)  
   3) Appraisal (if applicable)  
2) Value, Desire, and Commitment \- “Sell the Car”  
   1) Suggest/Select (product knowledge/charisma with customer to land on vehicle/backups that fit needs)  
   2) Explore (walkaround, sell the car- “features AND benefits to customer”)  
   3) Test Drive (demonstrate how it fits the needs)  
   4) Trial close (Is this the right car? If so park over here in our sold row and we will talk numbers. If customer declines, park back in available inventory. Mental seed planting)  
3) Win / Win Agreement \- “Sell the Deal”  
   1) Dealership tour (builds value in professionalism, value of working with)  
   2) Early Manager Intro (shows managers aren’t just behind the desk, customer service skills impress customers)  
   3) Agree (this is the paperwork step)  
4) After sale \- “Excited Loyal Lifetime Customer”  
   1) Delivery (salesperson impresses customer with showing them how to use what's important to them on their new car)  
   2) Follow up (consistent follow up for service, sales, referrals)

Buyer constraints or “Fears”

1) Buying the wrong car \- Directly related to salespersons skill for 1b, understanding goals.  
2) Paying too much \- Directly related to 2a, selecting wrong car (as well as poor salesmanship not building value)  
3) Being pressured \- Directly related to poor salesmanship, wrong car, etc.

Buyer purchasing threshold

1) VALUE has to EXCEED PRICE  
2) SALESPERSON IS REQUIRED TO BUILD VALUE  
3) MUCH EASIER TO BUILD VALUE WHEN CORRECTLY SELECTING VEHICLE, GOOD COMMUNICATION, GOOD EXPLORE (2b), GOOD TEST DRIVE (2d).

A closed customer that has properly gone through this process and value exceeds cost will have a lower resistance to F\&I product/profit plus future service/retention.

A closed customer that has skipped steps or not gone through the process correctly will have much higher resistance to further profit (F\&I, service, future sales, etc)
